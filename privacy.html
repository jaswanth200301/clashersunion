Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Clashers Union by Rowdy is owned and run by Rowdy. Questions about this policy: contact@clashersunion.tengosolar.com.

What we collect today

The site has no accounts yet. We do not ask you for any personal information, and we do not run analytics or set cookies. Our hosting providers (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) may process technical data, such as your IP address, to deliver pages and keep the site secure.

When accounts launch

To create an account we plan to collect only what we need: your player name, player tag, current clan name and tag, email address and a password. Passwords are stored in a protected form that we cannot read. We will also store what you post, such as chat messages. We check your player tag with Supercell's official game API to confirm the account belongs to you. We will update this policy before accounts open.

How we use it

To run your account, show your public player profile, operate chats, keep the community safe and contact you about your account. We do not sell your information.

Who helps us run the site

Service providers that host our database and send our emails process data for us only to provide those services.

Age

You must be at least 13 to use Clashers Union. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before signing up. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think we have, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing us. When accounts launch you will also be able to delete your account from your settings.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
